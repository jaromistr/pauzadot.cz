Support.

Something not working, got an idea for a new game, or just want to say hi? Write to hello@pauzadot.cz. We usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, the game and difficulty, and what happened (a screenshot is great).

Common questions

Does the app need the internet?

No. All games work fully offline – on a plane, on the subway or with no signal at all.

Is the app really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.

How do I play?

Every game has a “How to play” guide on its start screen. If you get stuck, a hint shows the next move and explains why: the dashed outline is the reason, the ring in the game’s color is the target.

How are stars counted?

For speed. Every puzzle has a target time: ★★★ within the target, ★★ within 1.5×, ★ within 2.5×. Each hint adds 15 seconds. In last. you play against an opponent, so there are no stars.

Where are my stars and stats stored?

Only on your phone. If you delete the app, they’re deleted with it. They only come back if you restore a full iPhone backup.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later.

How we handle data is described in our privacy policy.
